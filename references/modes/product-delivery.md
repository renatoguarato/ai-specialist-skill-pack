# Product Delivery Modes

## requirements

Convert a business need into observable outcomes, constraints, acceptance criteria, non-goals and solution options. Compare deterministic and AI approaches.

## interview

Create high-value technical discovery questions and identify what can already be answered from the repository.

## adr

Write an ADR with context, options, decision, trade-offs, consequences and unresolved assumptions.

## poc

Define hypothesis, scope, dataset, baseline, metrics, risks, timebox and decision rule for a proof of concept.

## experiment

Design controlled experiments for prompts, models, retrieval, agents or architecture, including variables, controls and reproducibility.

## translation

Translate technical findings into business impact, constraints, risks, decisions and next actions for non-specialist stakeholders.

## business-value

Assess value, cost, adoption, operational impact, risk and measurable success before recommending an AI initiative.

## documentation

Generate technical documentation from validated evidence, preserving source links, confidence and unresolved questions.

## meeting

Prepare context, decisions needed, evidence, questions and follow-ups for a technical meeting.

## ticket

Turn a ticket into clarified scope, affected areas, acceptance criteria, risks, dependencies and next investigation.

Follow `references/operating-contract.md` and keep deliverables concise and decision-oriented.
